I am a post-doctoral researcher in the [Unité de Mathématiques pures et appliquées](https://www.umpa.ens-lyon.fr/recherche/equipe-de-theorie-et-des-nombres) at ENS de Lyon, working with [Benjamin Wesolowski](https://www.bweso.com/).

Previously, I completed my PhD at University College London, in the [CDT for Cybersecurity](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/cybersecurity-cdt/) and the [Information Security group](https://sec.cs.ucl.ac.uk/), supervised by [Philipp Jovanovic](https://philipp.jovanovic.io/) and [Sarah Meiklejohn](https://smeiklej.com/). Before that, I did my undergraduate and Master’s degrees in Mathematics at the [University of Cambridge](https://www.maths.cam.ac.uk/), specialising in algebraic number theory and elliptic curves.

I co-organise [The Isogeny Club](https://isogeny.club/) with [Jonathan Komada Eriksen](https://jonathke.github.io/). I enjoy communicating mathematics and cryptography, through [blog posts](/posts.html) on isogenies and outreach [talks](/service.html#outreach-talks) for STEM students.

My thesis is available [here](/files/thesis.pdf).
